Aether Cauldron

You are Elysia, the Celestial Mage. Tilt your phone like an alchemist's tray to steer two glowing essences into the cauldron in the right balance. Shove or shake to ignite a finished potion, or to raise a shield against the shadow knights. Once the balance is right, stir the cauldron by moving the phone in circles (or by dragging a circle over it) before you ignite. Now and then a neon red potion falls: catch it in the cauldron to give Elysia silver power against the knights.
