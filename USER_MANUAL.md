# Argyria User Manual

## 1. Open Argyria

1. Open the published Argyria web link in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
2. Allow the page to finish loading.
3. Optionally install it from the browser's **Add to Home Screen** or **Install app** command.
4. Use the bottom navigation to move between Home, Play, Wellness, Relax, Rewards and Settings.

## 2. Home

The Home screen shows a daily introduction, a personal wellness snapshot and shortcuts to selected experiences. The snapshot reflects entries saved in this browser; it is not a clinical health assessment.

## 3. Play the games

Open **Play** to see all eight experiences:

- **Motion Lab:** live movement and supported device-sensor exploration.
- **Aether Cauldron:** fantasy alchemy game; follow its on-screen instructions.
- **Zone Runner:** movement-oriented runner game; follow its on-screen controls.
- **Relaxation Sanctuary:** choose a one-, two- or three-minute breathing guide. Breathe comfortably; do not force or hold your breath. Stop at any time. Optional audio can be played with the audio controls.
- **Nutrition Quest:** answer four general nutrition questions and read the explanations.
- **Crystal Focus:** watch the illuminated crystal sequence and tap the same sequence. Each correct round adds another item.
- **Garden of Vitality:** view your virtual collection and create a garden sparkle. Record water, sleep or steps in Wellness to earn related keepsakes.
- **Rhythm of Motion:** tap the glowing orb during its active beat window in a 30-second round. Touch interaction is supported; body movement is optional.

Use the **Argyria Home** link at the top of existing game pages to return to the main application.

## 4. Route Lab

Route Lab is a shared movement-tracking service, not a game. Open it from the Play page. Grant location permission only when you want to record a route. GPS requires a secure HTTPS website and location permission. GPS accuracy varies with device, environment and signal conditions. Review the on-screen distance, time, route and step-source labels. Sensor-estimated steps are estimates, not guaranteed exact counts.

## 5. Wellness journal

All entries are optional.

- **Mood:** select the word or emoji that best describes how you feel. Argyria offers a supportive suggestion, not a diagnosis.
- **Water:** enter the amount in millilitres and choose Record water. The amount is a self-reported record; the virtual sparkle is a game reward.
- **Sleep:** enter hours slept and optionally select a quality description. This is a personal log, not a sleep measurement.
- **Steps:** set a personal goal and enter a step count. Manual entries are labelled as manual. They are distinct from GPS distance and sensor estimates.

## 6. Rewards

Open **Rewards** to see unlocked collectibles and achievements. Rewards are not removed for missed days. A game achievement does not prove that a real-world health activity occurred.

## 7. Settings and privacy

Argyria stores its main journal, preferences and rewards in browser local storage on the current device. It does not require an account. Use **Export my data** to download a JSON backup. Use **Delete all local data** to erase the Argyria records stored by this browser. This action cannot be undone. Route Lab may maintain its own saved sessions separately.

## 8. Permissions and troubleshooting

- Use HTTPS (GitHub Pages provides HTTPS). Local `file://` opening is not suitable for GPS, motion permissions or PWA installation.
- If motion is unavailable, use the game's touch controls where offered.
- If GPS is unavailable, check browser location permission, device location settings and outdoor signal conditions.
- Some iPhones require a user gesture before motion permission can be requested.
- Audio may require pressing Play because mobile browsers restrict autoplay.
- Map tiles, external libraries and web fonts may require an internet connection. Offline availability is limited; offline maps are not guaranteed.
- If a page looks stale after an update, close and reopen it or clear the site's cached data.

## 9. Health and safety

Argyria is a wellness-awareness and entertainment prototype, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, prevent or treat health conditions. Stop any activity if uncomfortable. Choose comfortable movement and breathing. Seek qualified professional advice for personal medical concerns.
